Desktop microinteractions: as built and proposed

Left: the desktop as it is. Right, with the green outline: the same screen with the proposal. Hover, press and Tab through the right-hand frames yourself; they are live. The phone is not touched.

Every value is a motion token: --dur-fast .12s for hover and press, --dur-base .2s inside a component, --dur-slow .32s for a window arriving.

1. Header: hover, press, focus

Job: answer. Now the pill's fill snaps on hover. Proposed: it fades in over .12s; a press sinks; on Tab the focus ring is there at once (WCAG 2.4.7) and only settles 2px outward.

2. Left rail rows and the home's service cards

Job: answer. Hover a row or a card, press it.

3. Operator home: actions and the job groups

Answer on the action rows (.12s); connect on the group tabs: the selected fill moves to the tab you pick over .2s.

4. A window arrives

Job: connect. Now a step's window is simply there. Proposed: it rises 12px and fades in over .32s on the enter curve. Under reduce motion it only fades (WCAG 2.3.3). Tab roots (Activity, Jobs) do not move: switching tabs stays instant (HIG · Tab bars).

5. Loading, then the content arrives

Status, then connect. The skeleton pulses while loading (built). Now the loaded screen simply replaces it. Proposed: the window stays where it is, and the content fades in over .2s where the placeholders were.

6. The skeleton leaves in place

Status. On payment the window turns into Payment confirmed after 3s. Now the grey lines vanish in one frame. Proposed: they fade out over .2s on the exit curve.
