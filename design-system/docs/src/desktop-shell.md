- `.dr-mapground` — The ground of the whole window, the header included: the live map (see [Map ground](map-ground.html)).
- `.dr-veil` — Darkens the map towards its edges so what floats on it reads: a radial wash of the page colour, 10% at the centre, 40% at 70%, 75% at the edge (`--veil-map-*`; charcoal at 15 / 45 / 80% in dark).

- `.dr-deskrail--start` — A 300 rail 24 from the left edge, from 96 down to 24 above the bottom, its cards 12 apart (one group): what is happening for the client, the day for the operator. A short window scrolls the rail, never cuts its last card.
- `.dr-deskrail--end` — The same rail at the right edge: the client's orders, or the operator's availability and jobs.
- `.dr-panel` — The content in the middle, between the rails: a tab root's [panel](desktop-panel.html) — here Activity as [hanging cards](hanging-cards.html) — or a step's [window](desktop-window.html).
- `.dr-talkdock` — Talk to AI waits centred, 24 above the bottom, on every signed-in screen. It is not drawn where the assistant itself is on screen (the homes).

Every desktop twin stands in this frame (`wireframes/desktop/`, 135 pages): signed in, with the rails and Talk to AI; signed out, with the header and one window. ## Rule

The map is always the ground and everything floats on it: the header, the two 300 rails, one panel or window in the middle, Talk to AI at the bottom. Navigation is the header's. The first Tab stop is `.dr-skip`, *Skip to content*, linking to the page's one `<main id="content">`: out of sight until it holds the focus, then a solid pill under the logo (`--bg-material-solid`) with the 2.5 focus ring (`WCAG 2.4.1`, met; rev 335, the designer: «роби варіант A»).

## Anti-rule

Do not stretch the phone screen to the window: no status bar, no tab bar, no phone column, no home indicator. Do not write a media query on a screen: the desktop is its own twin page that consumes the system.
