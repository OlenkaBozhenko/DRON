1. Error: payment declined

Apple Pay couldn't complete the charge. Nothing was taken from your account. Try again or use another method. Calm and plain. Now the error page arrives like a new screen: the whole window rises 12px over .32s. Proposed: the window stays, the message appears in .12s from 4px.

2. Error on the map: tracking lost

We've lost the live signal from the operator's device. Your order is still active. Now the note is simply there. Proposed: it appears in .12s from 4px.

3. Empty: no operator free

All nearby operators are on jobs. The next one is likely free in about 40 minutes. Now: the window rises like a new screen. Proposed: the window stays and the content fades in, no movement.

4. Loading: the charge is in progress

Now: the loading page rises like a new screen. Proposed: the window stays, the content fades in, then the built loops run: the skeleton pulses 1 → .5 → 1 over 1.4s ease-in-out and the spinner turns linear, an even repeat with no jerk (checked in the keyframes).

5. Success: the money is sent

₴1,500 sent to your card. Visa •••• 3318. It usually shows within a minute. Now (rev 345): the content fades in, no movement. Proposed: it rises 12px and settles over .2s on the soft curve.

6. Success said in place: payment confirmed

After 3s the loading window turns into Payment confirmed. ₴180 paid with Apple Pay. Finding your operator. Now the words swap in one frame. Proposed: the confirmed block settles in, success tone.

7. List → detail: a job opened from the rail

On the operator's screens the right rail lists Your work. Now a job opened from it rises in the centre like any window. Proposed: it comes in from the right, the side the list is on, 12px over .32s.
