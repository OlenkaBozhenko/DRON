Forms 10

The largest gap in the system, and the one whose ground rules are now settled. Nothing a person types into, checks, picks or uploads has been painted — not one field on any of the three painted screens. Two rules landed on 2026-08-02 and the rows below follow them. First: a field has no border and lives in a row panel, which is where --r-input 10px finally landed — on the one field that keeps a ground, the search bar. Second: shape says how many — a circle is one of a mutually exclusive set, a square is an independent on/off. That is HIG · Radio Buttons / Checkboxes verbatim, and it is also the fix for the one real defect the count turned up: payment and withdraw carry type="radio" inside a square box, so the same shape means “one of these” there and “several of these” on operator-profile-setup — WCAG 3.2.4 Consistent Identification. The wireframes are not being changed for it; the rule is recorded and applied when those screens are painted. What is still owed on each row is the disabled state, and on the pickers the checked fill.

Nothing in the product floats. Counted across all 40 screens: no modal, sheet, popover, dialog, scrim or backdrop, and not one position:fixed. Every surface sits in the flow, which is why the flat rule has never had to answer for an overlay and why --sh-onphoto — the shadow named for a mark over pixels it cannot predict — is still spent nowhere. Where it is expected: a modal, a popover, a drawer or a dropdown menu — there a floating surface is appropriate and the exception is what it was named for. Nothing in the product is one yet.

Loading 4

Chrome stays solid, content pulses. A surface the loaded screen also draws is painted at full strength; a block the load replaces animates. The same rule gives opposite answers on the two painted loading screens, and that is the point — the question is never “should this pulse” but does what loads replace this surface, or sit inside it.

Four motion entries, and the chevron did not add a fifth. Three are loops that carry information — oh-ping 1.8s, skPulse 1.4s, skUp 1.6s — and one is the button’s transition, which answers a touch. The disclosure chevron’s 180° turn (decided 2026-08-02) is the second kind, so it takes the button’s transform .12s rather than an easing of its own.

The finding worth sitting with: the two most developed components in the whole system — the order card and the tracking timeline — are both here. Everything painted about them is bound to a single screen by its class names.

order-history The live and delivered order card, whole. Its drone frame, the status indicator that rides on it, the four-node horizontal rail, the From ▸ To inset strip, the fee row and the circular card actions — settled 2026-08-02 as the form of an icon-only button, being the only one on a painted screen — 28 classes, every one prefixed ohl- or oh-. The most measured component in the product and the least portable. Promote first if a second list screen is ever painted.
tracking The vertical milestone timeline — .timeline, .tl-item, .tl-dot, .tl-text — plus the map’s acquiring state and the late-notice wash. The node inside it is byte-identical to the order card’s, which is the argument for promoting one shared node rather than two.
listings-filters The filter sheet — .fgroup, .txt, .reset. .radio and .opt left this list 2026-08-02: counted as a control rather than as a class name, the radio stands on three screens and is a kit component now. This screen is the only one that draws it correctly.
listings-filtered The applied-filter row — .fchip, .count-badge, .applied, .clear-all, .x. A dismissible chip is close enough to the kit’s filter chip that the two should probably be one component before either is painted.
rate The five-star rating input — .rating, .star. Distinct from the rating value on the operator card, which is in the kit and painted. A filled star cannot be green: the accent is not an icon.
delivery The upload mark and its motion — .sk-up, .sk-arrow, .waiting. Painted, measured, and used once. The upload zone in the kit is where it wants to go next.
job-offer The offer card header — a lone .card-top that no other screen uses, though the painted order card has the same row under a different name.
withdraw The payout note — .check-note, a caption under a checklist row. One line of copy with its own class.
welcome Skip — .skip. The onboarding escape hatch; a tertiary button by another name.

How to read this page

  • A component earns a row by repeating. Two screens or more, counted across all 40 screens with their state pages collapsed — -empty, -error and -loading are states of a screen, not screens. Anything on exactly one screen is in One-off and stays out of the kit.
  • Painted rows are live and measured. Every specimen marked painted is the real CSS from the three painted screens, read off computed style — hover a button, tab to it. Nothing is a screenshot.
  • Grayscale rows show nothing, on purpose. A specimen for a component marked grayscale would have to invent the values it displays. The row says what the component is and what has to be decided instead.
  • Specimens do not follow your theme. The system is light-only, so a specimen that recoloured itself would be lying about what it documents. The chrome around them does follow it; in dark it takes direction 03 «Nocturne»’s graphite #14161A, recorded in concept.md as the alternative ground.
  • Sources — DESIGN.md for the values, concept/concept.md for the reasoning and the change log, wireframes/_screens.md for which screens and states exist at all.

This page is a snapshot, and a snapshot expires. Counted and measured on 2026-08-02 against 84 files. The wireframes are edited by hand and they are the design — so when a component changes there, or a fourth screen is painted, this inventory is wrong until it is regenerated. Do not treat it as the source of truth; treat it as a view of one.
