The Tone-Step Rule. Separation between two surfaces is a warm tone step plus a radius, and the step is measured before it is used. A surface boundary is a decorative edge — not text (1.4.3), not a control or state indicator (1.4.11) — so no contrast minimum binds it.

The No-White-No-Black Rule. Even the shadows are warm: every one is rgba(20,18,15,…).

The Fill-Only Rule. Green is a fill, a tint or a ring. As a foreground it is 1.69:1 on the page and 1.52:1 on the card, so it is never text and never a stroke. Anything sitting on green is --ink.

The Two-Ink Rule. A string is --ink or --slate. There is no third text colour and no colour-coded text of any kind.

Zero text-contrast failures across all ten painted surfaces; the lowest measured pair is 4.77:1 against AA's 4.5:1.

States

Message block.dr-msg · __title · __body · __actions · .dr-mark

One component doing three jobs — empty state, error banner, notice. Every string here is from microcopy.md.

No orders yet Your orders appear here, ready to re-book in a tap.
Live tracking lost We’ve lost the live signal from the operator’s device. Your order is still active.
Running a few minutes late Traffic near the drop-off is slowing your operator. They’re still on the way.

role="alert" on an error, role="status" on an empty state.

State tone, from voice.md. Empty → why it is empty plus one way out. Error → what happened and what to do, no apology and no joke. Loading → name what is loading. Success → the fact and the next step, no celebration. No dead ends: every error and empty screen carries a visible exit.

Padding 22px 18px, gap 12, body capped at 260px, actions full-width and stacked with gap 10.

Loading.dr-sk-line · .dr-sk-well · .dr-sk-block · .dr-sk-arrow · .dr-hint

Chrome stays solid, content pulses.

Waiting for the delivery photo…

The Skeleton-Measures-The-Load Rule. A loading block is the size of the thing it waits for: the order-history skeleton card is 263.0px against the delivered card's 263.6px; the delivery photo skeleton is 341 × 180 against a photo of 341 × 180. A skeleton is a claim about the loaded screen, so it expires when that screen changes treatment.

The rule gives opposite answers by design. The question is never “should this pulse” but “does what loads replace this surface, or sit inside it”.

Only the arrow leaves the tray, because what the screen waits on is a file going up. It rests visible at 0% and 100%, so a dropped animation leaves a legible icon. 1.6s is the cadence of an upload, not of a spinner.

Well.dr-well · --photo · --map · --acquiring

Real renders and photographs, never illustration and never a gradient.

Two treatments, one rule. A transparent cutout sits in the layout and is contain-ed — in the product with no ground at all, straight on the card. A photograph is the surface: cover, edge to edge, no padding. A photo floated inside a well reads as a sticker, so the two never swap.

Never upscale. Every box is a downscale, and the page serves a thumbs/ copy at roughly 2× the CSS box while the master stays in the repo.

Alt text names the outcome, not the equipment — “Your parcel on its way”, not “quadcopter with lime cargo box”. Decorative renders take alt="".

Do not recolour or gradient-map: the lime already is the accent. No added drop shadow — each render carries its own lighting.

A field has no border. WCAG 1.4.11 governs a boundary that is drawn, not one that is absent — so the failure was the grayscale 1px --line at 1.21:1, which does not survive painting. HIG got there first: the native form row is label, value, separator, no box.

So a field lives in a row panel and is the row that accepts typing. Value --ink 14.37:1 · label and placeholder --slate 5.95:1 · focus 2.5px --ink 14.37:1 · error --danger-ink 6.14:1, carried by words or a glyph, never by a boundary alone.

The search field is the one exception and keeps a ground — it is not in a grouped list. A filled rounded rect on the page, --media at --r-input 10px, which is the iOS search bar. That finally gives --r-input its only home.

Every row measures exactly 44.0px, so HIG's target is met by the panel's existing rhythm with no change.

Checkbox · radio.dr-pick · .dr-box--check · .dr-box--radio

Shape says how many. A square is an independent on/off; a circle is one of a mutually exclusive set.

checkbox · several at a time
radio · exactly one

HIG · Radio Buttons / Checkboxes says exactly this. iOS diverges — it has no radio control and marks one-of-many with a trailing checkmark — but DRON is mobile web in a browser with a WCAG 2.1 AA desktop, so the platform convention is what a user reads.

The unchecked outline is --slate, not --line. An empty box is identified by its boundary alone, so 1.4.11 applies at 3:1: --line is 1.21:1 and fails; --slate is 5.95:1 and passes.

Checked is charcoal, not green. --ink on --card is 14.37:1, and the screen's one green control is its action, not its picker. The green alternative measures 9.46:1 and remains open in ui/inventory.md §4.

The whole row is the 44pt target; the box does not have to be.

In-page tabs · filter chip.dr-groups · .dr-grouptab · .dr-chip

A row of places with one current — the tab bar's problem, so the tab bar's values.

At rest transparent with a --slate label, 6.62:1. Selected --green-wash with an --ink label, 14.75:1, at --r-pill. The fill step is only 1.085:1; the ink going --slate → --ink is the second signal.

The 44pt fault is fixed without thickening the row. The pill stays 32px visible and the hit area extends to 44 with a transparent ::after — the same thing iOS does with its own 32pt controls.

4.1.2 fixed too: the wireframe's <a href="#" aria-current> is a link, not a tab, and exposes no tab set. Here it is role="tab" in a role="tablist" with aria-selected.

Left open, deliberately. operator-listings runs the tab bar, these tabs and a segmented control at once, where HIG · Tab bars assigns those roles differently. Painting does not fix a structural call; it belongs to that screen's rework.

Disclosure.dr-disclosure · __item

A native <details>. It opens in flow and pushes the page down.

Client Client Operator

Nothing in this product floats. Counted across all 40 screens: no modal, sheet, popover, dialog, scrim or backdrop, and not one position:fixed. That is why the flat rule has never had to answer for an overlay and why --sh-onphoto is spent nowhere.

The selected row carries a checkmark, not the word “current” — --ink on --card, 14.37:1. This is the disclosure's rule; a form asking a question takes the circle instead.

The chevron turns 180° on open. It joins the button's transform .12s rather than becoming a fifth motion loop. Under reduced motion the glyph still turns — it just does not travel: the direction is information, the animation is only how it gets there.
