Rules of use one accent · no gradients over photography
  • Transparent PNG, never a rectangle. The drone sits in a surface — media well, radius 13. Never a bordered photo tile, no white box, no frame.
  • Do not recolour, tint or gradient-map. The lime already is the accent; a second green breaks the one-green-per-screen budget.
  • No added drop shadow. Each render carries its own lighting and contact shadow; a CSS shadow under a transparent PNG shows the canvas, not the drone.
  • One drone per well. A grid of drones is a specification — this page. In the product a screen shows the one unit doing the one job.
  • Scale down, never up. For the fleet, 1024² is the master. Below roughly 96 px the rotors stop resolving — use drone-12 or a line icon instead.
  • Never upscale a cutout. The scene cutouts are native, and some are small — drone-flight-clean is 409 × 174. Past native size they soften. Where a card needs a large image, reach for a fleet render or a scene.
  • One register per screen. Studio-on-a-payload and in-flight-in-use are visually different fleets. Catalogue and pickers get the fleet; live and outcome screens get the scenes.
  • People are not decoration. operator.png exists to answer RJ-C1 — it belongs where trust is claimed. Never background texture, never cropped so the face and the controller are both lost.
  • Alt text names the outcome, not the equipment (P1 / P4): “Your parcel on its way”, not “quadcopter with lime cargo box”. Descriptive alt is correct only here, where the asset is the subject.
Source figma · nodes 19:59139 + 38:122

The fleet — node 19:59139 «Drons images», one transparent 3072 × 2048 sheet in a 4 × 3 grid. Sliced on the alpha channel (runs of fully transparent rows and columns) so every cut lands in the gap and never on a rotor, then trimmed and centred on a square canvas.

The scenes — node 38:122 «images 2», four images. Here each subject is isolated by its own connected alpha component, dilated slightly so anti-aliased edges survive — not by bounding box. That is not fussiness: in receive the client's fingertips and the base of the hanging cargo box share a 20-pixel band, so a box crop would have left a slice of lime box floating above her hands. Component masks make a cutout structurally incapable of carrying a fragment of its neighbour; the spray plume survives for the same reason.

Both source sheets are kept beside the cuts as _source-sheet.png and _source-flight-sheet.png, so any crop can be redone without going back to Figma.
