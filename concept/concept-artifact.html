01

Designer’s taste

Taste comes first — every attribute below must agree with it. Likes are from the designer’s own Notion inspiration board; CoffeeTech is the strong like.

Likes — and the exact move to take
CoffeeTech
THE STRONG LIKE

Dark ground, white text, one warm accent used sparingly, generous whitespace, real product photography — no gradients, minimal-border cards with soft shadow, SVG icons.

Take — real photography over decoration; one sparing accent; minimal border + soft shadow; technical-but-warm restraint.

Don’t take — its dark-by-default ground and orange accent (see A-5).

Fauna Robotics
CLOSEST TO DRON’S JOB

Light ground, charcoal text, muted accent; real photo + demo video of the robot working; soft-border card grid; a flat icon on every capability; trust signals via client logos.

Take — light + charcoal + muted accent = calm; real photo/video; a flat icon per feature; trust signals shown openly.

Boiler Lab
FIRST ON THE BOARD

Bold sans headline, hard-number stat blocks (“20M+ users”), product cards with real screenshots.

Take — the bold headline and hard numbers as credibility (matches voice P2).

Don’t take — dark ground, neon, gradients (hit the anti-references).

Anti-references — what I definitely do not want
  • Warm cream background paired with terracotta — the AI cliché. (DRON’s canvas is warm oat; we stay clear only because the accent is green, not terracotta — see A-5.)
  • Gradients standing in for photography — decorative blobs / 3D abstract art instead of real content imagery. Real photos only.
  • Screens without icons — no icon-less text walls; every service, action and status carries a clear icon.
02

Attributes

Five pairs of visual opposites. Each = a data line + a borrowed technique from this concept; a pair without both is fiction. None contradicts the taste above. Where data and taste pull apart, it’s flagged ⚠ you decide. (Personas read from research/target-audience.md — there is no personas.md.)

A-1Calm, not anxious.

Data jtbd.md EJ-1 — handing control feels “safe, not reckless… someone accountable, not just available”; C-1 Bolt/Glovo model (“diverge in the first 60 seconds → they leave”); voice.md P5 — calm, never cheerful.

Technique graft G-5 (N26 single-accent × Perplexity accent-only-for-active) — ~one green element per screen; ~95% oat + charcoal + hairline.

✓ agrees with CoffeeTech / Fauna restraint.

A-2Trust proof up front, not buried.

Data jtbd.md RJ-C1 — “see proof of who will come — credentials, insurance, job history — without digging for it” (highest single drop-off); C-1 trust trigger — photo + rating on the card without a tap; voice.md P2 — show the proof, not the adjective.

Technique graft G-1 (Airbnb) badge on the avatar + G-2 stat trio beside the name, before any tap; Fauna’s openly-shown trust logos.

✓ agrees (Fauna surfaces credentials).

A-3Real photograph, not gradient decoration.

Data anti-reference — gradients instead of photos = no; jtbd.md RJ-C4 — proof of work is “a photo, a report, a confirmation”; voice.md P1 — name the real outcome.

Technique CoffeeTech + Fauna real photography/video; DRON anchors screens with the operator’s real photo (G-1) and the real delivery photo / inspection report — no 3D blobs.

✓ this pair is the anti-reference (strongest fit).

A-4Iconed & scannable, not an icon-less text wall.

Data anti-reference — screens without icons = no; C-1 icon-driven Bolt/Glovo model; jtbd.md RJ-O2 — gloves + sunlight → large targets, high contrast.

Technique Fauna flat icon per capability; CoffeeTech SVG icons. DRON: a flat icon per service and per status step (G-3 nodes already carry checks).

✓ Decided — build the icon setIcon system defined: flat monochromatic line icons, 24-grid / 1.75px, currentColor, green only as a fill behind the icon. SF Symbols on mobile + a matching custom set on web. Sample set below.

A-5Warm but grounded — not cream-and-terracotta, not cold.

Data CLAUDE.md tone — “technological, precise — not cold”; base = Monarch warm oat #efecea; anti-reference — warm cream + terracotta = no.

Technique Monarch warm-oat canvas + one green accent; CoffeeTech warmth-via-photos-plus-one-accent; grounded by Operate hairlines + charcoal + tabular numbers.

✓ Decided — keep both themes Ship both light and dark; the app follows the device appearance (Apple HIG), with a manual toggle — light is the Monarch/Fauna register, dark is the CoffeeTech register. The warm-oat canvas stays (the accent is green, not terracotta). Toggle this page’s theme, top-right, to see both.

03

The three-word brief

The direction had to satisfy three pulls at once — the search query that started the research. Most drone competitors in research.md pick one and drop the others.

Warm & classic

An established institution, not a gig app. A calm oat canvas and a serif-toned headline do this — the opposite of “Delivery, elevated.”

Calm & trustworthy

One accent, used about once per screen. Proof over decoration — the field’s one honest habit (numbers, not adjectives) kept; its spectacle dropped.

Precise & technological

Hairline structure, tabular figures, monospaced data labels. Technology shown through precision, never through drone jargon.

G-5 — from N26 + Perplexity

How much colour: about one accent element per screen

N26’s single-brand-colour discipline read through Perplexity’s “accent only for active states”: the CTA or the active node or the badge — not all three loud at once. Canvas, charcoal and hairlines do ~95%; accent ≤ ~5% of pixels.

A quiet screen reads as an institution you can hand control to; a loud one reads as a marketing app. Trust means no “plus fees” surprise, no shouting.

G-6 — from Operate (optional · operator SRM)

Hairline inset borders instead of shadows

For the data-dense operator dashboard (WCAG AA), rows separate with inset hairlines rather than soft card shadows — ledger-like, precise, lighter to render.

Keeps the “technological / precise” half of the brief for the people who work in it all day, without adding chrome.

Token seed & the accessibility correction

Warm base from Monarch, one green — #9bcf4a — for everything. It’s a light hue, so it can’t legibly be a text colour on the cream canvas; the rule is green is always a fill or highlight, with charcoal text on top. That keeps a single green everywhere and holds WCAG AA.

09

What we deliberately did not copy

Copying a whole reference is how a product ends up looking like everything else. The discipline is in the subtractions.

  • Not Monarch’s orange, nor a white-on-accent button — one salad-green #9bcf4a, used as a fill with charcoal on top (8.83 : 1), never as text.
  • Not Airbnb’s pink brand hue or full profile layout — only the badge placement and the stat trio.
  • Not N26’s teal or 4px sterile cards — only its single-accent discipline.
  • Not Perplexity’s sidebar/search shell — only its accent-for-active-state rule.
