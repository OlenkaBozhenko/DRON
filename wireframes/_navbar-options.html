The nav bar reads as two names

Measured on ten painted screens: the back label and the title are the same colour (#1A1A1A) at the same weight (600), 2px apart in size. And the title is never centred — it flows after the back label, so it drifts from −82.7px to +30.4px off centre depending on how long that label is. Nothing marks one as a control and the other as the name of this screen, so they read as two names of equal rank. Each option below is the real ui/kit.css tokens at production size, shown on the longest and the shortest pair in the set.

Nothing changes. Title flows after the back label; both #1A1A1A at 600.
